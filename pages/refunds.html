Refund policy

How cancellations, trials and refunds work for the paid plans of Cooking Daddy, Ohara, Can I See The Music, Show Me The Money and All-Access.

Last updated: [DATE]

Draft, not yet in effect. This page is a working draft for review before any paid plan opens. Text in [SQUARE BRACKETS] still needs filling in.

Cancel anytime

Cancel from your account page in one click (it opens the Stripe customer portal). Your plan stops renewing, and you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

Free trials

Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing. You get a reminder email three days before the first charge.

Monthly plans

Monthly charges aren't refunded for partly used months, but cancelling stops all future charges. If you were charged in error, or forgot to cancel and haven't used the app since the renewal, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] within [7] days and I'll refund it.

Yearly plans

Ask within [14] days of a yearly charge (the first one or a renewal) and you get a full refund. After that, the plan runs to the end of the year without renewing.

If something goes wrong on my side

If an app is unavailable for a long stretch, or I retire a paid app, I'll refund the unused part of your period.

Your legal rights

If you live in the EU, the UK or another place with a statutory right to withdraw from online purchases, those rights apply in addition to this policy.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe, usually within 5–10 business days. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

Privacy policy

This policy explains what Cooking Daddy, Ohara, Can I See The Music (Resonance Rings), Show Me The Money and Neuro Compass (together, the "apps") collect, why, and what you can do about it. The controller is [LEGAL NAME], [ADDRESS].

Last updated: [DATE]

The short version

  • Each app stores only what it needs to work for you.
  • Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach my servers.
  • No advertising trackers and no selling of data.
  • Neuro Compass stores nothing outside your browser.

Account and billing

Account: your email address and sign-in records, kept by Supabase (the account provider).

Billing: Stripe processes payments, and keeps your card details, billing address and tax information. I receive your subscription status, plan and renewal dates, not card numbers.

What each app stores

  • Cooking Daddy: body stats you enter (sex, age, height, weight, activity level) to size portions; the recipe cards and images made for you. Your requests are sent to OpenAI to write cards and make photos.
  • Ohara: the CV, profile links and text you provide; your research profile, chosen subjects, saves and votes. Text is sent to a language-model provider ([LLM PROVIDER]) to build your profile and feed.
  • Can I See The Music: audio files you upload for analysis and the stems and scores made from them, deleted after 30 days unless saved to your library. The live microphone view runs in your browser; that audio is never uploaded.
  • Show Me The Money: tickers, holdings, plan amounts and settings you enter, and the results computed from them.
  • Neuro Compass: nothing. Your answers stay in your browser's local storage on your device.

Why I use it

To run the apps you asked for (contract), to bill you and keep tax records (legal obligation), and to keep the service secure and fix problems (legitimate interest). I don't use your content to train models.

Who else processes it

  • Stripe (payments, tax and fraud checks)
  • Supabase (accounts and database)
  • Hosting: Vercel and Render
  • OpenAI and [LLM PROVIDER] (only for the AI features of Cooking Daddy and Ohara)
  • [EMAIL PROVIDER] (account and billing emails)

How long I keep it

App data is kept while your account is open and deleted within 30 days of closing it. Uploaded audio is deleted after 30 days. Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires.

Your choices and rights

You can view, export, correct or delete your data from your account page, or by emailing [SUPPORT EMAIL]. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law) you may also object to or restrict processing and complain to your data-protection authority.

Cookies

The apps use only cookies and storage needed to keep you signed in and remember settings. This website itself uses no cookies or analytics.

Changes

I'll post changes here and email account holders about significant ones. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

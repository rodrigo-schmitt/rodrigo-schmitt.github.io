Neuro Compass

A private, bilingual (English and Brazilian Portuguese) self-reflection quiz for ADHD, autistic and OCD traits, with every formula and research source on show. Your answers never leave your device.

Open the app

Free forever. No account, no tracking, no paywall.

Not a diagnosis. Neuro Compass is a reflective self-assessment to help you decide whether to talk to a professional. Only a psychiatrist, psychologist or neuropsychologist can diagnose ADHD, autism or OCD.

The idea

Online screeners tend to be either clinical forms with no explanation or quizzes with no grounding. Neuro Compass sits in between: 40 questions paraphrasing the constructs of validated instruments (ASRS, AQ-10, OCI-R), scored with the formulas shown openly on its Method page.

Results come as three scores with their uncertainty, and a trait fingerprint that puts all 13 sub-dimensions on one 0–100 scale. A one-page A4 report is ready to take to an appointment.

Screens

What's in it

  • 40 questions in about 8–10 minutes; progress saves automatically.
  • Every formula, cut-off and research source is on the Method and Research pages.
  • Light and dark themes, in English and Brazilian Portuguese.
  • Works fully offline once loaded, and can be installed on a phone.
  • Answers live only in your browser's storage; nothing is sent anywhere.

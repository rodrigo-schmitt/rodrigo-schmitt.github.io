Cooking Daddy

Recipe cards that scale every ingredient and every calorie to how much you really eat. Describe a craving and it writes a printable three-page card, with AI photos of the dish, each ingredient and each step, sized to your appetite.

The idea

Meal-kit cards are written for a standard serving, and almost nobody eats a standard serving. Cooking Daddy starts from your own body stats and appetite, so a recipe that serves four tells you it makes 2.5 of your meals, with calories and protein per meal set against your day.

Every ingredient shows two quantities side by side: the standard amount and yours. In the steps, ingredients are printed in green and tools in amber, so you can scan what each step needs.

Screens

What's in it

  • Cook: describe a mood or name a dish; a bar shows how one of your meals compares with your day.
  • Library: every card you've made, with filters by cuisine, time and protein.
  • Plan: this week's cards and one combined shopping list.
  • Cook mode: one step at a time, a checklist for each step, timers, and the screen kept awake.
  • Print: exactly three landscape pages (Front, Method and Numbers) for the fridge.

Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Every plan is cancelled in one click from your account page, which opens the Stripe customer portal. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

What happens when a free trial ends?

You're reminded by email three days before. If you do nothing, the plan renews at the price shown next to the button. If you cancel during the trial, you're never charged.

Can I take more than one trial?

One free trial per person for each app, plus one for All-Access.

What's the difference between the demo and the trial?

The demo needs no account and runs on sample data, so you can look around. The trial is the full app with your own data, within the trial limits.

Is there a student discount?

Ohara's Scholar plan is 50% off with a .edu email address.

Do you offer refunds?

See the refund policy.

What do the apps store about me?

Only what each app needs to work; card details stay with Stripe. The privacy policy lists it app by app. Neuro Compass stores nothing outside your browser.

Which currency?

Prices are in US dollars. Sales tax or VAT is added at checkout where it applies.

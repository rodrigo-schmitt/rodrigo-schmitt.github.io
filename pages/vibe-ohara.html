Ohara

Reads a researcher's CV and profiles, maps the subjects they're ready to learn next, and builds a feed of papers, books and talks, each with a one-line reason it is there.

The idea

Keeping up with a field means reading the right ten papers, not the newest thousand. Ohara starts from what you have already done: it parses your CV and public profiles into a research profile, proposes subjects that build on it, and lets you pick the threads you want.

The feed then pulls from open sources such as arXiv, OpenAlex and Open Library. Every item says why it was chosen, and your saves and "more like this" votes reshape the next feed.

The library of Ohara, in One Piece, is the island of scholars; this one is a reading room.

Roll with it
Temaki are all about momentum: keep nori sheets crisp, sushi rice warm-but-not-hot, and fillings ready. Work one roll at a time so the nori stays snappy. If you like, tuna or cooked shrimp for variety. Light, even pressure makes tidy cones.

Worth knowing
Sushi-grade salmon
Look for salmon labeled sushi- or sashimi-grade—flash-frozen at sea for quality and safety, then thawed under refrigeration. Its rich, buttery texture shines against cool rice and crisp veg. Keep it well-chilled and slice with a very sharp knife for clean, gorgeous batons.
*Fish is fully cooked when internal temperature reaches 145°.
1Cook rice
  • Rinse 3/4 Cup sushi rice (1 1/2 Cups for 4) in a fine-mesh strainer until water runs clear; drain 1 minute to dry.
  • Bring 1 Cup water (2 Cups for 4) and rinsed sushi rice to a boil in a saucepan. Cover, reduce heat to low, and cook 15 minutes. Remove from heat; rest 10 minutes, covered, for tender, separate grains.
2Season rice
  • In a small bowl, dissolve 1 TBSP sugar (2 TBSP for 4) in 2 TBSP rice vinegar (4 TBSP for 4) with 1/4 tsp kosher salt (1/2 tsp for 4).
  • Transfer hot sushi rice to a wide bowl; fold in vinegar mixture with a spatula until glossy. Fan to cool 5–10 minutes, then cover with a damp towel so the rice stays supple for rolling.
3Prep fillings
  • Keep Salmon cold; slice 14 oz (28 oz for 4) into 1/2-inch batons with a very sharp knife. Pat dry and return to the fridge until rolling.
  • Cut 1/2 cucumber (1 for 4) into matchsticks; thinly slice 1 scallion (2 for 4); slice 1/2 avocado (1 for 4). Thaw 4 oz edamame (8 oz for 4). Season lightly with kosher salt.
  • Keep seafood chilled and work clean; use a separate board for Salmon if preferred.
4Mix sauce
  • Stir 2 TBSP mayonnaise (4 TBSP for 4) with 1 tsp sriracha (2 tsp for 4) in a small bowl; add more sriracha to taste.
  • Toast 1 TBSP sesame seeds (2 TBSP for 4) in a dry pan over medium until fragrant, 2–3 minutes; transfer to cool.
5Set up
  • Halve 4 nori sheets (8 for 4) crosswise to make rectangles. Keep a damp towel over the warm sushi rice.
  • Arrange Salmon, veg, edamame, spicy mayonnaise, toasted sesame seeds, and tamari for dipping within reach. Keep unused nori sheets sealed so they stay crisp.
6Roll & serve
  • Place a nori sheet half rough-side up on your palm. Press a small mound of sushi rice on the left third; top with Salmon, cucumber, edamame, scallion, and a smear of spicy mayonnaise. Sprinkle sesame seeds; fold into a cone. Serve immediately with tamari.
